EMBERFALL : IDLE GAME RPG

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Emberfall : Idle Game RPG (“the game”) is an offline role-playing game. This policy explains what is stored on your device and what may be processed when advertising or Google Play services are used.

Data we collect

The game itself has no accounts and does not operate its own analytics or crash-reporting service. Your save files and settings remain in the app’s local storage on your device.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google and its partners may process the Android advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, and to personalise ads where you consent. In regions where consent is required, an in-game consent form is shown and can be revisited under Settings → Ads → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or limit ad personalisation in Android settings.

See Google’s advertising policy for more information.

Purchases

The one-time “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. The game receives only the purchase entitlement needed to remove ads; it never receives payment-card details.

Local storage and backups

Your hero saves, preferences, and exported backup codes are stored locally. Uninstalling the game deletes local saves. If you choose to share or export a backup, that action is controlled by you through the Android share sheet or clipboard.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

Besides Google AdMob and Google Play Billing, the game contains no social or analytics SDK. Share and Rate buttons open standard Android or Google Play surfaces only after you tap them.
